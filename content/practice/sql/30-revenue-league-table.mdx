The panda shop's owner wants a **league table** of customers 🏆. List **all 20 customers**, including the ones who haven't ordered yet or whose orders were all cancelled. Show:

- `name`: the customer's name,
- `revenue`: the total of their orders that **weren't cancelled** (quantity × unit price), or **0** if there are none,
- `share_pct`: their share of the shop's total revenue, as a percentage **rounded to 1 decimal place**. If the shop has **no revenue at all**, this is `NULL` (no division by zero!),
- `revenue_rank`: their place by revenue, highest first. Equal revenues **share a place** and the next place **is skipped**, like a sports table (1, 2, 2, 4).

Sort by `revenue_rank`, then by name.

```sql
-- name        | revenue | share_pct | revenue_rank
-- Ravi Patel  |  282.23 |      11.5 |            1
-- Sam Ortiz   |  273.83 |      11.2 |            2
-- …
-- Grace Kim   |       0 |       0.0 |           18
```

Build each customer's revenue first, then add the share and the rank with window functions.

<Hint>

In a CTE, start from `customers` and `LEFT JOIN` the orders, putting `o.status <> 'cancelled'` **in the `ON`** (in a `WHERE` it would throw away customers without orders), then `coalesce(sum(…), 0)`. In the outer query, `sum(revenue) OVER ()` is the shop's total on every row; divide by `nullif(…, 0)` so an empty shop gives `NULL`, and rank with `rank() OVER (ORDER BY revenue DESC)`.

</Hint>

<Solution>

```sql
WITH spend AS (
  SELECT c.id, c.name,
    coalesce(sum(oi.quantity * oi.unit_price), 0) AS revenue
  FROM customers AS c
  LEFT JOIN orders AS o
    ON o.customer_id = c.id AND o.status <> 'cancelled'
  LEFT JOIN order_items AS oi ON oi.order_id = o.id
  GROUP BY c.id
)
SELECT name, revenue,
  round(100 * revenue / nullif(sum(revenue) OVER (), 0), 1) AS share_pct,
  rank() OVER (ORDER BY revenue DESC) AS revenue_rank
FROM spend
ORDER BY revenue_rank, name;
```

</Solution>
