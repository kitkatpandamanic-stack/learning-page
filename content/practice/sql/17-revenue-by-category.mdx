The panda shop's buyers want to know which shelves earn their keep 💰. For each product **category**, show:

- `category`,
- `units`: how many items of that category were sold,
- `revenue`: the money they brought in, `quantity * unit_price` added up.

Only count items from orders that were **not cancelled**. Sort by `revenue`, **highest first**, then by `category`.

```sql
-- category | units | revenue
-- kitchen  |    33 | 1160.10
-- books    |    35 |  830.41
-- …
```

Join the order items to their orders and products, drop cancelled orders, and group by category.

<Hint>

What you need is spread over three tables. Which one has the quantities and prices, which has the order status, and which has the category?

</Hint>

<Hint>

Start from `order_items`, then join `orders` on `o.id = oi.order_id` and `products` on `p.id = oi.product_id`. Drop cancelled orders with `WHERE o.status <> 'cancelled'`. Then `GROUP BY p.category`, with `sum(oi.quantity)` for `units` and `sum(oi.quantity * oi.unit_price)` for `revenue`. Sort by `revenue DESC, p.category`.

</Hint>

<Hint>

```sql
SELECT p.category,
  sum(oi.quantity) AS units,
  ... AS revenue
FROM order_items AS oi
JOIN orders AS o ON o.id = oi.order_id
JOIN products AS p ON ...
WHERE ...  -- leave out cancelled orders
GROUP BY p.category
ORDER BY revenue DESC, p.category;
```

</Hint>

<Solution>

```sql
SELECT p.category,
  sum(oi.quantity) AS units,
  sum(oi.quantity * oi.unit_price) AS revenue
FROM order_items AS oi
JOIN orders AS o ON o.id = oi.order_id
JOIN products AS p ON p.id = oi.product_id
WHERE o.status <> 'cancelled'
GROUP BY p.category
ORDER BY revenue DESC, p.category;
```

</Solution>
