The panda shop is starting a loyalty card 🐼, and the first cards go to the **regulars**: customers with **at least 3 orders that weren't cancelled**. Show:

- `name`: the customer's name,
- `orders`: how many of their orders weren't cancelled,
- `first_order`: the **date** (not the time) of their first such order,
- `last_order`: the date of their last such order.

Sort by `orders`, **most first**, then by `name`.

```sql
-- name         | orders | first_order | last_order
-- Lucas Silva  |      3 | 2025-01-28  | 2025-06-17
-- …
```

Count each customer's orders that weren't cancelled, and keep only the customers with 3 or more.

<Hint>

Filter single rows before grouping with `WHERE o.status <> 'cancelled'`, then `GROUP BY c.id, c.name`. Filter the groups with `HAVING count(*) >= 3`. `min(o.ordered_at)::date` gives the date of the first order.

</Hint>

<Solution>

```sql
SELECT c.name,
  count(*) AS orders,
  min(o.ordered_at)::date AS first_order,
  max(o.ordered_at)::date AS last_order
FROM customers AS c
JOIN orders AS o ON o.customer_id = c.id
WHERE o.status <> 'cancelled'
GROUP BY c.id, c.name
HAVING count(*) >= 3
ORDER BY orders DESC, c.name;
```

</Solution>
