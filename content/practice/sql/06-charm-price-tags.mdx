The panda shop is printing new shelf labels 🏷️. Shoppers love prices that end in **.99**, so the owner has a rule: take the price **with 20% VAT**, **round it up** to a whole number, then take off **0.01**.

For every product that costs **less than 20** (before VAT), show:

- **`name`**;
- **`price`**: the price as it is;
- **`with_vat`**: the price times 1.2, **rounded to 2 decimal places**;
- **`price_tag`**: the price with VAT **rounded up** to a whole number, **minus 0.01**.

Sort by **`price_tag`**, cheapest first; if two tags are the same, sort by name.

```sql
-- name          | price | with_vat | price_tag
-- Rice Crackers | 2.50  | 3.00     | 2.99
-- Bamboo Crisps | 2.99  | 3.59     | 3.99
-- …
```

Work out the VAT price and the .99 price tag for each product under 20.

<Hint>

`round(x, 2)` rounds to two decimal places, and `ceil(x)` rounds **up** to the next whole number. You can do arithmetic on the result, like `ceil(…) - 0.01`.

</Hint>

<Solution>

```sql
SELECT
  name,
  price,
  round(price * 1.2, 2) AS with_vat,
  ceil(price * 1.2) - 0.01 AS price_tag
FROM products
WHERE price < 20
ORDER BY price_tag, name;
```
