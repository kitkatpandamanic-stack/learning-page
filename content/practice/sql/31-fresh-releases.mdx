The panda film club has three new films to add to `movies` 🎬. Add them with **a single `INSERT`** that lists its columns:

| title | year | director | genre | rating | minutes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Dumpling Detectives | 2025 | Mei Lin | comedy | 7.4 | 99 |
| Orbit of the Red Panda | 2025 | Kenji Mori | sci-fi | 8.0 | 126 |
| Paper Lanterns | 2024 | Ada Brooks | drama | 7.7 | 110 |

Then finish with a query that shows the **`title`**, **`year`** and **`director`** of every film from **2024 or later**, newest year first. Films from the same year are sorted by title.

```sql
-- title                  | year | director
-- Bug in the Machine     | 2025 | Ada Brooks
-- Dumpling Detectives    | 2025 | Mei Lin
-- …
```

Add the three films with one `INSERT`, then list the films from 2024 onwards.

<Hint>

One `INSERT` can add several rows at once if you give it several groups of values. After that, the query needs a filter on `year` and a sort on two columns.

</Hint>

<Hint>

Name the columns once, `INSERT INTO movies (title, year, director, genre, rating, minutes) VALUES …`, then write one pair of brackets per film, separated by commas. Leave out `id`: the database numbers new rows itself. After the `INSERT`, filter with `WHERE year >= 2024` and sort with `ORDER BY year DESC, title`.

</Hint>

<Hint>

```sql
INSERT INTO movies (title, year, director, genre, rating, minutes) VALUES
  ('Dumpling Detectives', 2025, 'Mei Lin', 'comedy', 7.4, 99),
  ...;  -- the other two films
SELECT title, year, director
FROM movies
WHERE ...
ORDER BY year DESC, title;
```

</Hint>

<Solution>

```sql
INSERT INTO movies (title, year, director, genre, rating, minutes) VALUES
  ('Dumpling Detectives', 2025, 'Mei Lin', 'comedy', 7.4, 99),
  ('Orbit of the Red Panda', 2025, 'Kenji Mori', 'sci-fi', 8.0, 126),
  ('Paper Lanterns', 2024, 'Ada Brooks', 'drama', 7.7, 110);

SELECT title, year, director
FROM movies
WHERE year >= 2024
ORDER BY year DESC, title;
```

You don't give an `id`: the column is a `serial`, so the database numbers the new films itself (13, 14 and 15).

</Solution>
